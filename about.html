What interests me

My focus is the digital implementation flow: writing and verifying RTL, synthesizing it, and taking it through floorplanning, placement, clock-tree synthesis, routing, timing closure, and physical verification to GDSII. I want to understand how choices in the RTL and implementation flow affect timing, area, and power.

I’m also interested in FPGA architectures and the tools used to implement them. My research in Sharc Lab involves 3D FPGAs and changes to VTR and OpenFPGA. Alongside my RTL-to-GDSII work with SiliconJackets, it gives me two closely related areas to keep exploring: implementing digital designs and developing the programmable hardware they can run on.
